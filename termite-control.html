What Are Termites?

Termites are small wood destroying insects that live in colonies and feed on cellulose found in wood and furniture. They are often called “silent destroyers” because they work secretly inside walls, furniture and flooring. By the time homeowners notice termite damage, a large part of the structure may already be affected. Termite infestation is very common in Delhi NCR due to humidity and underground soil conditions.

Why Termites Are Dangerous?

Termites can cause severe structural damage to homes, offices and commercial properties. They attack wooden doors, wardrobes, beds, cabinets, false ceilings and even books. Over time termites weaken the strength of buildings and repairing the damage can cost lakhs of rupees. Early termite treatment is always cheaper and safer than repairing structural damage later.

Signs of Termite Infestation

  • Hollow sound in wooden furniture
  • Mud tubes on walls or corners
  • Wood powder near doors and windows
  • Swollen doors or jammed windows
  • Discarded termite wings near windows

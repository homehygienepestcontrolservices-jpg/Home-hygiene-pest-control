What Are Bed Bugs?

Bed bugs are tiny blood-sucking insects that hide in mattresses, beds, sofas and furniture. They usually come out at night and bite humans while sleeping. Bed bug infestation spreads quickly and can become very difficult to control without professional treatment.

Why Bed Bugs Are Dangerous?

Bed bugs cause itchy bites, red marks, skin allergies and sleepless nights. Their bites can lead to stress and anxiety due to lack of sleep. Bed bugs multiply very fast and one small infestation can become a major problem within weeks.

Signs of Bed Bug Infestation

  • Red bite marks on skin after sleep
  • Blood stains on bedsheets
  • Black spots on mattress corners
  • Itching and sleepless nights
  • Bed bugs seen in furniture joints

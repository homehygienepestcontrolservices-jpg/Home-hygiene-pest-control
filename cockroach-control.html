Why Cockroach Infestation Is Dangerous?

Cockroaches are one of the most common household pests in Delhi NCR. They live in kitchens, drains, bathrooms and food storage areas. Cockroaches carry bacteria and germs that contaminate food and kitchen surfaces. They can spread diseases such as food poisoning, allergies and asthma. A single cockroach seen during the day usually means hundreds are hiding in cracks and cabinets.

Signs of Cockroach Infestation

  • Cockroaches seen in kitchen at night
  • Bad smell in cabinets and drawers
  • Black droppings near kitchen shelves
  • Cockroach eggs near appliances
  • Allergy or breathing issues
